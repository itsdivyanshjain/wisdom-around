**Agency** refers to a capacity to act, and **High Agency** refers to our ability to put our conscientious efforts, choices, and actions into asserting ownership of our lives and responsibility for our actions.

## Being a God

During my college time, I was passionate about hacking, and I was learning about computer security, mainly how a computer system works and where it breaks. I then started tinkering with my personal system, random sites, and playing CTF. Eventually, I managed to get my first Security Vulnerability (IDOR) in my college website. At that time, I felt like I was in the driver seat of this universe. It felt close to being a god. I was able to generate favorable outcomes from my inputs.

This sense of controllable success is often mirrored in different ways in people’s lives. For instance, when they are consistent with the gym, with their passionate work, or with doing the hard things that matter to them. Basically, it is like noticing **the act of our will leading to such powerful progress that it doesn’t feel less than a God.**

Then life happens, as it happens for everyone: you may get sick, have a breakup, face layoffs, lose your loved ones, or simply go through a long vacation, get married, have a baby, or start a new job.

## Being a Human

Often, this makes you realize that you are not in your best shape because you are away from the gym or you are not able to perform your best work. This way, you feel like you are losing control.

Now, every attempt to regain control makes you more anxious, irritated, frustrated, and bitter. This causes you to be unnecessarily hard on yourself. Also, you start projecting yourselves to control others people lives. We often develop a tendency to bend every outcome through our actions and build a sense of righteousness. Like, when people around us do not act responsibly, or when their decisions lack values this action/inaction irritates us. Although we should hold people accountable for their actions, they are often just in survival mode, lacking awareness, and acting unempathetically towards our loved ones at those moments is worse.

If we focus on the bigger picture, we can see that we started by exercising high agency through action. But now, **we are taking actions simply to feel a sense of control over our lives.** Here, any uncertain outcome becomes unbearable for ourselves. At this phase of life, people often choose astrology, Kundli, Vaastu, and more to ease their pain regarding uncertain outcomes.

Sometimes we are dealt cards that feel bizarre, unfair, and painful. In such moments, we struggle with forcing ourselves to maintain control when all we need is space and patience to understand the situation. Being unkind to ourselves at this moment is disrespectful not only to our current selves but also to the version of us who worked hard to reach where we are now.

## Being a River

Each of us believes that things should be the way we want them, rather than the natural flow of life. From what I know, the only way out from the constant attempt to control and determine what will happen in our lives is through an act of acceptance and surrendering the need to control our life. I know it is easy to say this, but it is hard, especially when we have strong preferences or have cared deeply about something and are not getting what we want. I don’t know how people learn to accept or surrender. But I know that, sooner or later, they will realize that there is more to life than what just happened or what could have happened. This realization doesn’t negate what has happened or any fear or tension; it acknowledges it and simply pushes them forward to live the life that they still have.

![Image](/../../../images/blog/wisdom-around/beyond-the-agency-1.jpg)

I started this year with this quote. As a high agency person myself, practicing acceptance and surrender is the toughest pill to swallow, but it works. **Surrender feels soothing because we don’t feel performative, resistive, or anxious; it feels natural, like a flow.**

At this point, many people confuse surrendering to life with surrendering their will because they cannot differentiate between the two. That is why people often heavily resort to bhakti, prayer, and affirmations instead of actions. These surrender acts are important soothing complements to our actions, but they are not a replacement for them. Basically, we should try to respect the flow of life and then use our conscience, actions, and values to tame the flow, and finally, be open to the outcomes, because **“The great moments of your life won’t necessarily be the things you do; they’ll also be the things that happen to you.”**

## Being a High Agency Person

I didn’t mention detachment in this essay, and the reason is that I often cannot be indifferent to the outcomes. I have found it hard to detach myself from the outcomes when I care, but I know that **when you care enough, you get hurt greatly, and then you become numb to the pain.** This is what I define as detachment, compared to simply being always indifferent to the outcomes.

In retrospection, I’ve realized that you can indeed bend an outcome through your actions, but trying to bend every single outcome only opens the door to misery. So, be kind to yourself, fight the battle you care the most, and surrender to the rest.

**“Beyond the agency lies high agency, and that doesn’t mean to fully control yourself and others, but utilizing the agency to leap into the life. It is being in the flow in such a manner that your values, choices, and actions are in harmony.”**
